# Delivery - User Stories v1

### US-DEL-001: Track Delivery Partner
**As a** customer
**I want to** see my delivery partner's location
**So that** I know when they arrive

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Real-time map tracking
- Partner photo and name
- Phone call/message option
- ETA updates
- Refresh capability

### US-DEL-002: Delivery Instructions
**As a** customer
**I want to** provide delivery instructions
**So that** partner knows where to leave items

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Instructions text field (500 chars)
- Gate code entry
- Leave at door option
- Flat number entry
- Instructions sent to partner

### US-DEL-003: Delivery OTP
**As a** customer
**I want to** verify delivery with OTP
**So that** no one else receives my order

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- OTP sent to customer
- OTP shown to partner
- Partner enters OTP
- Verification success
- Proof of delivery photo

### US-DEL-004: Rating Delivery
**As a** customer
**I want to** rate the delivery experience
**So that** quality improves

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Star rating (1-5)
- Comment field (optional)
- Timeliness rated
- Professionalism rated
- Order completion feedback

### US-DEL-005: Delivery Issues
**As a** customer
**I want to** report delivery issues
**So that** problems can be resolved

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Issue type selection
- Photo upload
- Description field
- Immediate response option
- Chat with support

### US-DEL-006: Failed Delivery
**As a** customer
**I want to** reattempt delivery
**So that** I receive my order

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Reason for failed delivery
- Reschedule option
- New time slot selection
- Confirmation sent
- No charge for reattempt

### US-DEL-007: Contactless Delivery
**As a** customer
**I want to** opt for contactless delivery
**So that** I maintain distance

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Contactless toggle
- Leave at location option
- Photo proof required
- OTP optional for contactless

### US-DEL-008: Delivery Receipt
**As a** customer
**I want to** download proof of delivery
**So that** I have record

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Photo of items shown
- Delivery partner signature
- Date and time
- Items list
- PDF download option
