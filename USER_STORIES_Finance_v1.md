# Finance - User Stories v1

### US-FIN-001: Vendor Settlement
**As a** vendor
**I want to** receive payment for my sales
**So that** I can run my business

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Weekly settlement (Every Friday)
- Automatic bank transfer
- Settlement report available
- Deductions shown: Commission, Returns, Chargebacks
- 12 months settlement history

### US-FIN-002: Settlement Breakdown
**As a** vendor
**I want to** see detailed settlement breakdown
**So that** I understand deductions

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Gross sales shown
- Commission % breakdown
- Return amount deducted
- Chargeback deducted
- Net amount calculated

### US-FIN-003: Payment Tracking
**As a** vendor
**I want to** track pending payments
**So that** I know when money arrives

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Pending orders shown
- Amount at risk (chargebacks)
- Expected settlement amount
- Settlement schedule shown

### US-FIN-004: Revenue Analytics
**As a** vendor
**I want to** see revenue analytics
**So that** I can track business growth

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Daily revenue
- Weekly trends
- Monthly comparison
- Top selling products
- Growth percentage

### US-FIN-005: Tax Calculation
**As the** system
**I want to** calculate tax correctly
**So that** compliance is maintained

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- GST as per product category
- CGST/SGST split
- Tax invoice generated
- Tax report for authorities

### US-FIN-006: Wallet Balance
**As a** customer
**I want to** view my wallet balance
**So that** I know available funds

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Current balance displayed
- Recent transactions listed
- Add money option
- Refund/Credit additions shown

### US-FIN-007: Wallet Top-up
**As a** customer
**I want to** add money to wallet
**So that** I can pay faster

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Top-up amount entry
- Payment method selection
- Instant credit to wallet
- Transaction receipt
