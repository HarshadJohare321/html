# Business Rules Catalog v1

Central catalog of marketplace business rules.

## Core Rule Areas
- Identity and access control
- Vendor eligibility and governance
- Catalog and inventory validity
- Cart, checkout, and order lifecycle integrity
- Payment, refund, and settlement controls
- Delivery and pickup execution controls
- Dispute resolution and liability handling
- Notification reliability and preferences
- Audit, retention, and traceability

Use this file as the single business-rules reference across all modules.
