# Business Rules Catalog v1

## Hyperlocal Grocery Marketplace - Kharadi, Pune

### Pricing Rules
- BR-001: Minimum order value: Rs 250
- BR-002: Maximum discount: 40% per item
- BR-003: Delivery charges: Free for orders > Rs 500
- BR-004: Tax calculation: GST as per item category
- BR-005: Price history maintained for 1 year

### Availability Rules
- BR-006: Products shown only in service area
- BR-007: Out-of-stock items hidden after 30 mins
- BR-008: Inventory updated real-time
- BR-009: Overselling prevented at checkout
- BR-010: Reserved items released after 15 mins if unpaid

### Delivery Rules
- BR-011: Standard delivery: 30-60 minutes
- BR-012: Express delivery available 7am-11pm
- BR-013: Same delivery partner assigned if return needed
- BR-014: Delivery location radius: 5km from hub
- BR-015: Delivery partner rated before order closure

### Return/Refund Rules
- BR-016: Return window: 7 days from delivery
- BR-017: Refund processed in 48-72 hours
- BR-018: Free return shipping
- BR-019: Item inspection photo required
- BR-020: Final refund after inspection

### Payment Rules
- BR-021: Payment timeout: 10 minutes
- BR-022: Failed payment retry: 3 attempts
- BR-023: Wallet maximum balance: Rs 50,000
- BR-024: Refund to original payment method
- BR-025: Transaction confirmation email mandatory

### Dispute Rules
- BR-026: Dispute filing window: 30 days from order
- BR-027: Resolution time: 7 days
- BR-028: Auto-approval if vendor non-responsive
- BR-029: Dispute types: Quality, Delivery, Payment, Other
- BR-030: Resolution evidence: Photo/Video/Invoice
